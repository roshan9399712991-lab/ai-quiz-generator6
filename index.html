About Our School

Government Sandipani School, Chhatwai, is a government co-educational institution located in Shahdol district, Madhya Pradesh.

Our mission is to provide quality education, develop creativity and encourage students to explore modern technologies.

We focus on academic excellence, practical learning, innovation and digital education.
